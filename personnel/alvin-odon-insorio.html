Alvin O. Insorio

Master Teacher II · Research leadership and Senior High School instruction

Professional Profile

Role in school research

Public research and professional sources describe Alvin O. Insorio as a Master Teacher II and research advocate who has guided SPRCNHS teachers and students in research writing, presentation, publication, and dissemination.

He is publicly associated with PROBE, the school's research journal of teachers and students, and with the SPRCNHS Virtual Research Hub.
