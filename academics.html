Current curriculum context, academic pathways, and SPRCNHS technical-vocational strengths.

SY 2026–2027

Strengthened Senior High School Curriculum

DepEd directed all public and private Senior High Schools to implement the Strengthened SHS Curriculum for incoming Grade 11 learners beginning SY 2026–2027. Grade 12 learners in non-pilot schools continue under the existing SHS curriculum for this school year.

Open the official DepEd SSHS page →
How the strengthened curriculum works

Flexible pathways instead of fixed strands

Core Subjects

The strengthened curriculum streamlines the core and is designed to strengthen foundational learning and career readiness.

Academic Track

Learners may select academic electives aligned with interests and goals rather than entering a single fixed strand.

Technical-Professional Track

TechPro electives are designed around employability, certification, practical skills, and industry-linked learning.

SPRCNHS Programs

Technical-vocational strengths of the school

Public school sources identify SPRCNHS as offering technical-vocational specializations across Home Economics, Industrial Arts, and Information and Communications Technology. Exact specializations available to a learner can change by school year.

Home Economics

Skill-based learning connected with hospitality, home economics, and related service occupations.

Industrial Arts

Technical learning connected with industrial, mechanical, electrical, fabrication, and related competencies.

ICT

Information and Communications Technology specializations and digital workplace competencies.

New in 2026

Tech-Pro Maritime

Public partner reporting identifies SPRCNHS as the first educational institution in Laguna authorized to offer the specialized Tech-Pro Maritime Senior High School pathway. The program is supported by the Sea Mariners initiative and maritime-industry partnerships.

Academic Programs

Junior and Senior High School Curriculum

SPRCNHS provides Junior High School Technology and Livelihood Education and Senior High School learning pathways. The categories below present the broad curriculum areas currently identified for the school; individual specializations and electives will be published after school confirmation.

Junior High School

Technology and Livelihood Education / TVE

SPRCNHS JHS currently identifies three major TVE/TLE areas.

JHS TVE / TLE

Home Economics

Practical learning related to home, family, livelihood, services, and entrepreneurship.

JHS TVE / TLE

Industrial Arts

Technology-based practical learning involving tools, materials, systems, fabrication, maintenance, and related industrial skills.

JHS TVE / TLE

Information and Communication Technology

Digital technology, computer systems, communication technologies, and related ICT skills.

Agri-Fishery Arts is part of DepEd's national TLE framework but is not listed here as an SPRCNHS JHS TVE area.
